# Safety and clinical-claim boundary

This repository does not calculate or certify maximum permissible exposure for a human eye.

A laser's average power alone is not enough. Retinal safety depends on wavelength, pulse duration, repetition rate, exposure duration, pupil size, scan pattern, angular subtense, accessible emission, optical losses, accommodation, and the exact edition and interpretation of relevant standards.

The project may report simulated optical parameters, but should not state that the system is Class 1, Group 1, “safe,” or below MPE without a documented calculation reviewed by an appropriate laser-safety and ophthalmic specialist.

Recommended manuscript wording:

> The present work is a computational imaging study and does not establish ocular safety or in-vivo feasibility. Any experimental retinal implementation would require an independent exposure analysis, instrument characterization, ethical approval, and biological validation.

Keep safety analysis in a separate protocol. Do not tune a physics simulation until it outputs a desired safety conclusion.
