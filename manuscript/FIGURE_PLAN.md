# Proposed figure plan

## Figure 1 - method and validation

- Optical/signal schematic.
- Three excitation pathways.
- Lock-in channel signature matrix.
- Direct time-domain versus analytic channel agreement.

## Figure 2 - retinal reconstruction

- Ground-truth morphology or measured phantom.
- Raw FE channels.
- Calibrated FE reconstruction.
- Conventional emission-filtered reconstruction.
- Blind NNMF reconstruction.

## Figure 3 - additive noise and photon budget

Show the regime where FE benefits from collecting all emission photons and the regime where shared shot noise removes that advantage.

## Figure 4 - failure regimes

- Signature condition number.
- Brightness imbalance.
- Spatial overlap.
- Detector bandwidth or pulse-overlap mismatch.

A strong discussion should explain failures rather than hiding them.
